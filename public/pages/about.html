Our Foundation

Built on Professionalism & Disciplined Execution

Headquartered in Sinza B, Dar es Salaam (P.O. Box 65000), Evatho Matrix Security Limited is an established Tanzanian private security company providing layered physical, tactical, and technological defense solutions.

Under our emblem—a circular tiger seal representing vigilance and courage—we deploy disciplined manned guards, armed escort teams, rapid mobile patrols, trained K9 units, metal detection technology, and dedicated emergency ambulance services.

Our Mission

To provide dependable, disciplined, and technologically equipped security solutions that safeguard individuals, physical assets, bullion, and corporate investments throughout Tanzania.

Our Vision

To stand as Tanzania's premier benchmark for private security excellence, characterized by accountability, zero compromise on integrity, and rapid tactical innovation.
