About Us

Company History

Evatho Matrix Security Limited was established in 2012 and registered in Tanzania under the Company Act 2002. We comply with all regulatory requirements and are a wholly owned Tanzanian firm dealing with various security activities across the country.

Our headquarters is located in Dar es Salaam, from where we coordinate our nationwide operations. With years of experience and a team of dedicated professionals, we have built a reputation for reliability and excellence in the security industry.

We pride ourselves on our commitment to safety, innovation, and customer satisfaction, making us the preferred security partner for businesses and organizations throughout Tanzania.
